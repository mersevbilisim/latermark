Auto-delete

Every frame knows when to leave.

Pick a lifespan as you capture. The clock starts at the shutter, not at the last edit, and when it runs out the note and its photograph go together — no archive, no bin, nothing to tidy up later.

Cleanup runs when the app opens, when it returns to the front, and once a minute while you are in it. A frame is never left sitting past its time.

Your data

It never leaves the phone.

Latermark processes your notes, photographs and location tags entirely on your device. There is no account to create, no server to sync with, and no analytics — nothing is uploaded, not for backup and not for "improving the product".

  • Nothing to sign up for There is no login, no profile and no email address. The app opens on your notes.
  • Reminders come from your device They are scheduled and shown by the operating system. No external service sends or triggers them.
  • Deleting the app deletes the notes Its frames and location tags go with it. Your photo library and other apps are untouched.

Backups are encrypted on the device with a password only you know. Lose it and the file cannot be opened again — not by us either.

Questions

The ones worth asking.

Does Latermark upload my photos?
No. Notes, photographs and location tags are processed entirely on your device. There is no account and no sync server, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it yourself.
What happens when a frame expires?
The note and its photograph are deleted together, on the device. The clock starts at the moment of capture, so editing a note later does not extend its life.
Does it change the photos in my library?
No. When you pick an existing photo, Latermark copies it into its own storage and leaves the original in your library exactly where it was.
Do reminders work if notifications are off?
The frame is still saved and the reminder is still stored, but it cannot ring. It starts working the moment you allow notifications.
Is Latermark Pro a subscription?
No. Pro is a one-time purchase with no renewal, and it stays yours.
