Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1 Introduction

At Latermark, operated by Mersev Bilişim Yazılım Ltd. Şti. ("we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and data when you use Latermark.

2 Information Collection & On-Device Storage

Latermark is designed with privacy in mind and operates primarily as an on-device application. Below is how we handle different types of data:

  • Photos and Personal Notes: Any photos captured/imported or notes added within the application remain strictly on your local device storage. We do not upload, backup, or transmit your photos or notes to external cloud servers.
  • In-App Purchases (IAP): We offer one-time digital product purchases. Financial transactions and purchase processing are handled directly and securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or payment credentials.
  • Local Notifications: The app utilizes local device notifications to deliver study reminders and alerts. These notifications are scheduled locally on your phone and do not rely on remote push servers or external tracking.

3 How We Use Information

Since your content (notes, photos, app progress) resides exclusively on your local device, it is used strictly to deliver core app functionality to you. We do not monetize, analyze, or profile your personal content.

4 Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Third-party interactions are limited to standard platform store billing operations (Apple App Store / Google Play Billing) required to process in-app purchases.

5 Children's Privacy

Latermark does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because all user-generated content remains locally on the user's device, no personal data is transmitted to us or third-party servers.

6 Data Security & Control

Your data security is guaranteed by your device's native operating system security standards. You retain full control over your data. You can erase all notes, photos, and app history at any time by clearing the application data or uninstalling the app from your device.
